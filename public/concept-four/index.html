What’s worth connecting?

Innovate Connects is a telecoms adoption consultancy. We help public and private-sector organisations decide where emerging connectivity can solve a real problem and what it will take for people to use it.

See how we work

From evidence to adoption.

For public bodies, businesses and telecoms teams working out where better connectivity can make a difference.

  • Research

    Work out what the technology can do, where it could help and what might get in the way.

  • Workshops

    Bring technical teams, decision-makers and the people affected into the same conversation. Agree on the problem and what to test.

  • Adoption strategy

    Decide what to prioritise, who needs to be involved and how to tell whether a trial is worth taking further.

Start with the problem, not the network.

What should connectivity make easier? Who needs to use it? What would convince you it’s working? Those questions give a first conversation a useful starting point.
