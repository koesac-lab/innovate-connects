One identity. Three expressions.

A coordinated artwork family for Innovate Connects: expressive ribbons for major brand moments, quieter companions for everyday content, and gold filaments for a lighter, more open treatment. This gallery is where we review the family together, not just individual images.

The plan

Build a recognisable visual system from the same flowing curves, fine strands and selective points of connection. Change the composition to suit the layout rather than repeatedly cropping or rotating one image. These are supporting brand graphics, not replacements for the logo.

Route 01

Signature ribbons

Rich petrol/teal and burgundy ribbons, selective metallic gold detail, intricate filaments and restrained luminous nodes. The most expressive version of the identity.

Use for website heroes, report covers, presentation openers and larger campaign moments. Preserve clear space for the headline.

Route 02

Quiet ribbons

The same shapes and palette with fewer strands, smoother shading, less texture and minimal glow. Simplify the artwork without losing its flowing character.

Use for everyday slides, document corners, supporting website sections and content graphics where the message should lead.

Route 03

Gold filaments and flares

Fine, individually defined gold threads and small crisp flares only. No solid ribbons, filled bands or translucent sheets. Let open space carry the composition.

Use for elegant framing, dividers and document backgrounds. On white, retain readable warm-gold threads and small flares: subtle through spacing, not a ghostly fade.

Six compositions, one visual language

  • Diagonal flow — the signature ascending sweep, leaving room for a headline.
  • Horizontal current — a shallow flow for banners, dividers and footers.
  • Vertical cascade — a purpose-designed portrait arrangement for covers and side panels.
  • Open arc — an incomplete organic curve with a clear centre for a message or key figure.
  • Corner flourish — a compact accent that leaves most of the page clear.
  • Convergence — separate streams joining gracefully into one, for partnership and adoption themes.

Use these as a composition framework across all three routes; not every route/shape combination is necessarily produced or approved yet. Judge the family on real white documents, dark slides and website layouts before expanding it further.

What to review

  • Consistency of curves, palette and filament detail across the family; avoid unrelated decorative motifs.
  • Real transparency, especially between fine threads. Check white, dark and custom backgrounds for haze, hard masks and coloured fringes.
  • Text space and contrast at the intended layout size. Gold flares should remain small, not compete with headings or body text.
  • Actual pixel dimensions, embedded DPI and edge quality at 100%. An image-generation draft is not automatically a finished print master.
