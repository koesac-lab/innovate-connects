Brand foundations

A visual language for connected ambition.

01 / Mark & wordmark

A circular mark with an outer orbit, inner connection ring and central signal point.

02 / Colour

Charcoal creates authority; dark teal anchors the connected-technology story; burgundy, gold and antique beige add warmth and contrast.

03 / Typography

A decisive serif perspective paired with spacious sans-serif copy and disciplined technical labels.

04 / Iconography

Fine-line, concentric and node-based geometry that feels like purposeful network diagrams.
