Four ways to
connect.

The same initials, four different ideas. These are exploratory marks, not a final identity. Each direction is designed to work as a symbol first, with the full name as a separate lockup.

01 / SignalDirect · recognisable

The signal is the I

Two Wi-Fi arcs take the position of the I dot. No additional dot, node or orbit: the signal and the letterform are one idea.
