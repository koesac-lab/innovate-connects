05 / Woven IC

Brief ready · artwork pending

Design a monogram for Innovate Connects, a wireless consultancy. The ribbon C expresses movement and connection; the solid I gives it a precise, stable centre. Aim for clarity in complex connectivity, not a generic Wi-Fi badge.

Art direction

Use the open-arc artwork for the C silhouette and the diagonal ribbon artwork for folds, filaments and highlights. Petrol teal dominates; oxblood is the underside and gold is a fine edge or filament. Keep the I matte: warm ivory on dark, deep petrol on white. Use the full name in the primary lockup, iCons as secondary shorthand, and keep any consultancy descriptor separate.

Copy-ready image prompt

Create a square, front-on logo concept for Innovate Connects, a wireless consultancy. Centre a compact, right-facing capital C made from one broad sculptural ribbon. Cross it vertically with a solid matte warm ivory capital I, slightly left of the centre of the opening. The C passes behind the I at the upper crossing and in front at the lower crossing. Leave the middle of the I exposed and continuous. Keep the I upright and within the vertical bounds of the C. Give the C a generous right-hand opening and subtly asymmetric tips.

Use deep petrol teal woven filaments, restrained oxblood underside folds, fine gold edge threads and two or three tiny warm luminous points integrated into the weave. Keep all texture inside the silhouette. The I is flat and untextured. Use a uniform deep charcoal background and ample clear space. Centre the exact wording “Innovate Connects” below the mark in an ivory contemporary mixed-case sans serif. No tagline, Wi-Fi icon, signal bars, floating particles, mockup frame or extra text.

Alternatives to test

  • Signal fold: keep the same IC silhouette and split one C terminal into two or three short, progressively offset strands.
  • Bar-built I: keep the C quiet and build the I from a compact stepped form with one dominant upright. Drop it if the I reads as a reception icon instead of a letter.

Review before production

  • Prove the geometry in one colour at 16 px, 32 px and site-header size, on white and reversed on dark. Check that it does not read as a power button, copyright mark or C with a slash.
  • Apply the ribbon treatment to the same silhouette. Use the rich version on a report cover and the quiet mark in the Concept Three header alongside the hero artwork.
  • Produce a flat SVG for everyday use and a rich render for larger placements. No artwork has been generated yet.
